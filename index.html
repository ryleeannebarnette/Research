WHAT WE DO

Three questions guide every project

STRUCTURE

Which crystals qualify?

We screen candidate crystal structures for the nonlinear-optical and symmetry properties required for spontaneous parametric down-conversion into three photons rather than two.

METHOD

How do we scale the search?

Currently, we are searching through Zinc based metal organic framework crystals (MOF) for their use in generating photon entanglement.

APPLICATION

What is it good for?

With technology and high performance based computing equipment advancing, sources of entangled photon triplets are a missing piece for certain photonic quantum-computing and quantum-communication applications — this lab works to supply acceptable candidate materials.
