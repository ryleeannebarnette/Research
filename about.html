About the lab & My Research Focus

EV

Dr. Sanoj

Assistant Professor · Department of Chemistry & Biochemistry · University of Central Arkansas

I'm a computational chemist working with computaitonal chemistry, metal organic crystal materials, and quantum theory My group asks a deceptively simple question — which crystals can split one photon of light into three entangled photons? — and builds the multiscale computational methodology needed to answer that question.

Before starting this lab I earned my Phd studying physical chemistry and quantum mechanical topics at the University of Chicago in Chicago, Illinois. I spent my Postdoc teaching at the University of Notre Dame teaching students and learning more about quantum and materials chemistry. I have much experience in materials chemistry, working on electronic-structure methods and calculations for nonlinear-optical materials.
