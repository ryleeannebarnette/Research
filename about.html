About the lab & principal investigator

EV

Dr. Elena Voss

Assistant Professor · Department of Chemistry · [University Name]

I'm a computational chemist working at the interface of solid-state chemistry and quantum photonics. My group asks a deceptively simple question — which crystals can split one photon of light into three entangled photons? — and builds the computational pipeline needed to answer it at scale, from first-principles physics up through machine-learned screening.

Before starting this lab I trained in computational materials chemistry, working on electronic-structure methods for nonlinear-optical materials. I now split my time between developing new theory, writing the software that turns that theory into a working pipeline, and mentoring the undergraduates who do a lot of that building with me.
