WHAT WE DO

Three questions guide every project

STRUCTURE

Which crystals qualify?

We screen candidate crystal structures for the nonlinear-optical and symmetry properties required for spontaneous parametric down-conversion into three photons rather than two.

METHOD

How do we scale the search?

First-principles calculations are accurate but slow. We connect DFT, lattice-dynamics, and trained surrogate models so thousands of candidates can be triaged before the expensive step.

APPLICATION

What is it good for?

Sources of entangled photon triplets are a missing piece for certain photonic quantum-computing and quantum-communication architectures — this lab works to supply candidate materials.
